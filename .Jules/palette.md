## 2024-05-18 - Tooltips for icon-only buttons\n**Learning:** Icon-only buttons with the `title` attribute for tooltips have poor accessibility, screen readers prefer `aria-label`, and the `material-icons-round` child span must have `aria-hidden="true"` to prevent double-reading of the icon name.\n**Action:** Replaced `title` with `aria-label` on icon-only buttons in `settings_access.html` and added `aria-hidden="true"` to their inner spans.
